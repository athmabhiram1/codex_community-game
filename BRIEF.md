# Tidewater: brief (draft)

**The idea.** A slow day on a tropical island, with a rod in your hand. Fish from the pier, the beach or your own boat, then sell your catch and spend it on better gear.

**What it's going for.**

- A world you want to stand in: a real-time ocean, a volcanic island, a reef to swim and a whale to spot.
- Fishing with some fight in it: strike when the bobber goes under, and keep the line tension in the green to land the fish.
- A reason to keep casting: 18 species that depend on the water and the time of day, a log of records, gear from the chandlery.

**What it isn't.** A rush. It takes a moment to load and a while to play, and that's the point.
